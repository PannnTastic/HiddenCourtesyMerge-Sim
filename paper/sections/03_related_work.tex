\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\subsection{Hidden Driver Intent and POMDP Planning}
Planning under partial observability is theoretically well established
\citep{kaelbling1998planning,kochenderfer2022algorithms}.
Sunberg and Kochenderfer \citep{sunberg2020internalstates} model hidden
driver aggressiveness as a latent variable and show safety benefits from
belief-based planning, while Kruse et al.\ \citep{kruse2022uncertainty}
plan under learned uncertainty over merge behaviour.
Li et al.\ \citep{li2021interactionaware} condition on inferred intent using
real traffic data, and Wang et al.\ \citep{wang2021uncovering} extract latent
states from naturalistic on-ramp trajectories.
None of these works provides matched counterfactual evaluation where all
policies share identical episode seeds with ground-truth courtesy labels.

\subsection{Merge Negotiation and Simulation Environments}
Reinforcement learning has been widely applied to on-ramp merging
\citep{chen2021deep,liu2022autonomous}, and social-attention approaches
\citep{leurent2019social} demonstrate interaction-aware representations in
highway-env without an explicit belief state.
The IDM \citep{treiber2000congested} and MOBIL \citep{kesting2007mobil}
models parameterise longitudinal and lateral driving behaviour through
interpretable quantities, making them well-suited for controlled courtesy
injection.
Highway-env \citep{leurent_highway_env} provides the lightweight simulator
used here; SUMO \citep{lopez2018microscopic} and naturalistic datasets such
as highD \citep{krajewski2018highd} offer richer settings but do not supply
verified latent type labels.

\subsection{Research Gap}
The key unmet need is a benchmark combining (i)~ground-truth binary courtesy
labels implemented via clean parameter injection, (ii)~a matched experimental
design in which every policy faces the same episode sequence, and
(iii)~calibrated belief estimation with rigorous paired hypothesis tests and
power analysis.
Naturalistic datasets satisfy (i) only partially and cannot satisfy (ii) or
(iii); prior POMDP simulation work satisfies (ii) and (iii) only informally.
HiddenCourtesyMerge-Sim is designed to satisfy all three simultaneously,
enabling the matched counterfactual evaluation left open by prior work.
